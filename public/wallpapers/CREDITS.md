# Chat wallpapers

Six photographic textures, sourced from Unsplash and served locally.

**Licence.** The Unsplash License permits use for commercial and non-commercial
purposes, free of charge, with no permission needed and no attribution
required. Credit is recorded here anyway — it costs nothing and it means the
next person can find the original rather than guessing where a file came from.

They are SELF-HOSTED, deliberately. Hotlinking `images.unsplash.com` would make
a chat background depend on a third party's uptime and CDN, and would leak
every viewer's IP to them on every page load.

Each is resized to 1600px and re-encoded at quality 62 — 872KB for all six,
served once and cached, against a chat surface people keep open all day.
